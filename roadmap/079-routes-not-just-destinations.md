# 79. Routes, not just destinations: the path to travel, with the obstacles that matter to you

## What

Given where you are and where you are going, show the path on a floor map and describe it as numbered plain-language steps with landmarks; never route over stairs when step-free is requested; avoid or warn about crowd and noise zones when asked; state every obstacle (stairs, heavy doors, long corridors, level changes, locked-after-hours doors) with the same verified or unverified marking as places.

## Why

Merge Forward: a destination without a route is half an answer; the research found the same gap in every group.
