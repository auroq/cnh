# 0004. Elasticsearch as the single store

Status: Accepted, 2026-11-09.

## Context

The app holds three kinds of document: sessions from the schedule, venue places and rooms, and access features.
Questions mix exact terms (room 260A, a speaker's name) with meaning ("somewhere quiet near the keynote").
The ECK operator was installed before kickoff, and the Elasticsearch track judges hybrid search.
A second database would mean a second thing to deploy, secure, back up and make Radar-clean on a one-day budget.

## Decision

Elasticsearch, run by ECK in the cluster, is the only store.
Text that people search by meaning goes in `semantic_text` fields on the default ELSER endpoint.
Queries are hybrid: BM25 and semantic retrievers combined with RRF.
"Nearest" is answered from level and a hand-set room adjacency stored on the documents, not from a geospatial route.
The ingest job is the only writer; the API reads.

## Consequences

- ELSER and `semantic_text` need a trial or paid license on a self-managed cluster, so the trial is started in item 006; it lasts 30 days, which outlives the event.
- ELSER needs an ML node of at least 4 GB on x86-64, and scales to zero after 15 idle minutes unless a minimum allocation is set.
- ELSER is tuned for English; other languages would need the E5 endpoint.
- Losing the cluster loses the data, which is acceptable because every document is regenerated by the ingest job.
